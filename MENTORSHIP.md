## 🧑‍🏫 Get Mentorship

Mentors will be available during the hackathon to guide teams in different areas of their project.

### 🛠️ Technical Development

Get guidance on:

- Project architecture
- Development approach
- Technical implementation
- APIs and integrations

### 🤖 AI / ML & AI Integration

Get guidance on:

- AI/ML models
- AI integration
- Choosing suitable AI technologies
- Using AI effectively in the solution

### 💡 Product & Solution

Get guidance on:

- Understanding the problem
- Solution approach
- Product direction
- Improving the proposed solution

### 🎨 UI/UX

Get guidance on:

- User experience
- Interface design
- Usability
- Product presentation

### 🎤 Presentation & Demo

Get guidance on:

- Demo preparation
- Explaining the solution
- Project presentation
- Demonstrating the working prototype

---

## 🤝 How to Use Mentorship

Come to mentors with a **specific question or challenge**.

Be ready to explain:

- What problem you selected
- What you are building
- What you have already tried
- Where you are stuck

> **Mentors guide. Teams build.**
