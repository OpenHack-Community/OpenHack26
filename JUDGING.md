## 📊 What Judges Look For

| Criterion | Weight | Judges will consider |
| :-- | :-: | :-- |
| 🛠️ **Technical Implementation** | **25%** | • How well the solution is technically implemented<br/>• Quality of the technical approach<br/>• Effective use of technologies, APIs, AI/ML, open-source tools, or other components where appropriate<br/>• Whether the team can explain how their implementation works |
| 🎯 **Problem & Impact** | **20%** | • How clearly the team understands the selected problem<br/>• How effectively the solution addresses the problem<br/>• The potential usefulness and impact of the solution |
| 💡 **Innovation** | **20%** | • Creativity of the solution<br/>• Originality of the approach<br/>• How intelligently technologies are combined to solve the problem |
| 🎨 **Usability & Design** | **15%** | • Ease of use<br/>• User experience<br/>• Clarity of the interface and overall solution<br/>• Whether users can understand how to use the product |
| 📈 **Feasibility** | **10%** | • Whether the solution is practical<br/>• Whether it could realistically be developed further<br/>• Potential for real-world use |
| 🎤 **Demo & Presentation** | **10%** | • How clearly the team explains the problem and solution<br/>• Quality of the demonstration<br/>• Ability to explain the technical implementation<br/>• How effectively the team presents and demonstrates the working prototype |
| **Total** | **100%** | |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **AI is allowed**, but teams must understand and explain their implementation.

## 📌 Important

* Judges will evaluate projects using the criteria above.
* Teams should be prepared to **demonstrate and explain their implementation**.
* AI and open-source technologies are allowed, but teams should understand the technologies they use.
* The submitted project should include a **working prototype** and required documentation.
