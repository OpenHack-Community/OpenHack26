## 🤖 AI Usage

AI tools are **allowed and encouraged** where they help teams build better solutions.

Teams may use:

- AI assistants
- AI/ML models
- AI APIs
- Generative AI tools
- AI-powered development tools

### ⚠️ Understand Your Implementation

Using AI is allowed, but teams must:

- Understand the code and solution they submit.
- Be able to explain how AI is used.
- Be able to explain important technical decisions.
- Disclose the AI tools or models used in their project.

> **AI can assist your work. Your team must understand and own the solution.**

---

## 🌐 Open Source

Teams may use:

- Open-source libraries
- Frameworks
- APIs
- Models
- GitHub projects
- Other open-source technologies

Use open-source resources responsibly and follow their applicable licenses.

---

## 📝 Disclosure

Teams should mention relevant AI and open-source usage in their project documentation.

Include information such as:

```text
AI Tools / Models:
- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- OpenAI API

Open-Source Technologies:
- React
- FastAPI
- TensorFlow
```
