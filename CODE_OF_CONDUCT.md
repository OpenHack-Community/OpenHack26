## 🤝 Our Community

OpenHack'26 is a collaborative environment where students, mentors, judges, and organizers work together.

Everyone is expected to:

- Treat others with respect.
- Communicate professionally.
- Respect different ideas and opinions.
- Help maintain a positive and inclusive environment.
- Respect mentors, judges, organizers, and other participants.

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Abusive, threatening, or offensive behavior.
- Intentionally disrupting another team's work.
- Misuse of another team's project or work.
- Any behavior that violates the official hackathon rules.

---

## 🛡️ Reporting an Issue

If you experience or witness inappropriate behavior during the hackathon, report it to an **OpenHack'26 organizer or coordinator**.

Organizers will review the situation and take appropriate action according to the hackathon rules.
